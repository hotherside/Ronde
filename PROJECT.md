# Ronde

Ronde is an independent Apple Watch golf companion for counting shots and reviewing a round, with a universal iPhone/iPad Shot Reviewer for local range-video review.

## Product promise

Start a round quickly, log one shot per physical action, see the current hole and score clearly, and retain a trustworthy local record after finishing. On iPhone and iPad, import fixed-tripod range footage, open a single swing directly into source-time-synchronised playback with an evidence-backed observed tracer or explicit no-tracer state, or divide longer recordings into non-destructive impact -5/+5 shot clips without cloud processing. A long recording may create an automatic clip and tracer only after the target golfer's impact and a stable, time-aligned golf-ball launch are both established; practice swings, generic motion, background sounds and another golfer's ball stay out of the shot rail. Every uploaded frame rate is accepted; source quality changes confidence, not eligibility. The free on-device tracker is integrated and proven on one supplied clip, but held-out accuracy and physical-device performance remain release gates.

## Current stage

Ronde is in pre-release product hardening and reviewer-foundation development. The current release candidate contains the reliability, Action Button, workout-recovery, persistence and visual pass for Watch. The reviewer source foundation is active on the current branch, while end-to-end capture, physical-device ML/tracer quality and distribution status still require direct verification. See [Current State](docs/context/CURRENT_STATE.md) before making release claims.
