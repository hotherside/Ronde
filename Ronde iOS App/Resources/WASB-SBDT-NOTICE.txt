GolfBallTracker is a Core ML conversion of the official WASB-SBDT tennis model.

Upstream project: https://github.com/nttcom/WASB-SBDT
Upstream model zoo: https://github.com/nttcom/WASB-SBDT/blob/main/MODEL_ZOO.md
Official PyTorch weight SHA-256: 9d391239ab10c733f8e5bfadf16ab72838e7a8ebc88e8ae2038501c03d42b4bb
Converted Core ML weight SHA-256: 508eec685ff6f8d20667d739ed0f3038a20d37de4121056a1b4f537c5564f8ee

The model runs entirely on device. Ronde sends no uploaded video or inference data to a server.
