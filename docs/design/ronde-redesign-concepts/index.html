03 · Personal alternative

Range Journal

A location-led record of where the golfer practises and plays. Sessions become memories anchored to a place, making repeat visits and favourites feel meaningful rather than merely counted.

Best at
Building emotional attachment and long-term retention
Signature
Place cards, topographic layers, and monthly field notes
Watch-out
Location entry must stay optional and privacy-safe
