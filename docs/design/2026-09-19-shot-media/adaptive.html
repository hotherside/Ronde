Room for the shot
and its context.

The session stays beside the selected shot. Studio actions move to the outer edge, preserving vertical space for media.

APPLE’S PATTERN

Outer display and inner landscape use vertical system bars. In a split view, the detail column participates; the collection keeps its own controls.

RONDE’S INTERPRETATION

Keep the library beside the preview, with edit actions near the media they affect. Selection and draft choices survive the transition.

Read the Apple guidance ↗
01

Space decides the layout.

Use the available scene, size classes and safe areas. A narrowed iPad window needs the same care as a compact phone.

02

The fold is conditional.

Keep important content and controls clear when partly folded. When the screen is flat, let the available space work.

03

Opening is not starting over.

Keep the same shot, favourite, draft and place in the source. Every action remains available in every pose.

This study does not establish native Duo support. Apple currently provides Xcode 27.1 beta for implementation and testing. System bars, fold regions, sheets, text sizes, camera transitions and physical-device behaviour remain separate validation steps. Apple’s Duo resources ↗
