A

Mark the moment.
Make the shot.

Watch a 10–20-minute recording, bookmark the swings you want and create the clips in a batch. The prototype starts with 5 seconds either side. Automatic suggestions can help with the search later.

B

A shot can be a keeper
without a trace.

Finding a possible shot and tracking the ball are separate steps. A confirmed shot stays editable and exportable when the ball is not tracked. Manual traces remain labelled.

C

One collection.
No duplicate originals.

A shot points to a time range in its recording. A bookmark is a timestamp; a keeper is a favourite shot. Creating more clips preserves existing edits and the original recording. Native long-video support remains future work.

SAME SHOT. MORE ROOM.

Expand the workspace.
Keep your place.

iPhone focuses on one step. iPad can keep the collection beside the editor. Duo adds side controls and fold-aware layouts, while preserving the selected shot and editing state.
